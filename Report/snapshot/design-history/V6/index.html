          02

          Exposure window

          Mosca's inequality: if X + Y > Z, data recorded today is readable before you have finished migrating.

          Assumptions

          2034

          Michele Mosca's formulation, and the standard way this risk is stated. X is how long the data must stay secret, Y how long migration takes, Z the years until a quantum computer exists. Nobody knows Z, so it is given as a range rather than asserted as a date — move the control and every asset re-ranks against it.

v11 — the assessment record, reorganised.

Chrome
  · Sidebar removed. Contents moved on to the masthead as a horizontal
    section nav with an underline for the current section.
  · Scan controls moved out of the masthead into their own full-width bar,
    with room for real field labels and larger inputs.
  · The sidebar's scan metadata became a "Scan record" block inside section 01,
    now also carrying the per-sensor file counts.

Scan telemetry
  · A full-width band with a 1.6rem percentage, the live phase, a 6px track
    with a moving hatch, and six sensor pills that change state as each
    sensor runs. Replaces the previous hairline strip.

Exposure window, reimagined
  · The inequality is stated first as a strip of labelled cells:
    X + Y = protect-through vs Z -> exposure, P(exposed).
  · One chart on one axis. Above it, the protection window as two bars.
    Below it, Q-Day modelled as a triangular distribution over
    [earliest, likely, latest]; the portion falling inside the protection
    window is shaded, and that area is the probability of exposure.
    Replaces the plain timeline.

Inventory
  · Two views on one toggle. "Ranked" is the default: one asset per row with
    risk drawn as a length and coloured by class, readable across a room.
    "Table" is the previous dense eight-column record. Filters, the class
    filter and Report / CBOM / Validate serve both.

Robustness
  · renderAll now isolates each renderer, so a fault in one figure cannot
    blank the rest of the document.

Zero external requests. No hex colours in app.js.

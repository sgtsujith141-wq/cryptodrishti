    Composition by quantum exposure class

      Scan record

      Six sensors read source, compiled binaries, certificates, dependency manifests, configuration and live endpoints. This is not a bug hunt — it is an inventory of every piece of cryptography present, classified by what a quantum computer does to it.

      02

      Risk distribution

      One mark per cryptographic asset, placed at its computed risk score. Marks stack where scores coincide.

      A distribution, not a ranking: it shows at a glance whether the estate has a few bad assets or is uniformly poor. Hover a mark for detail, click to open its evidence. Selecting a class in the key above filters both this plot and the inventory.

      03

      Exposure window

      Assumptions

      2034

      X is how long the data must stay secret, Y how long migration takes, Z the years until a quantum computer exists. Nobody knows Z, so it is modelled as a triangular distribution over the plausible range and reported as a probability — never asserted as a date.
